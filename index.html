QzQGeo

QzQGeo makes short educational history map animations (borders of countries and empires over time) for YouTube Shorts and TikTok.

The QzQGeo publisher is a private tool used only by the channel owner: after a video is finished, the owner presses a button in a private Telegram bot and the video is sent to the owner's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API, after the owner chooses who can see it.
